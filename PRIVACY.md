# Privacy

Better Growave has no analytics, telemetry, developer-operated server, or advertising.

It reads the wishlist rendered by Turntable Lab to display the enhanced view. It requests current product data and the existing session cart from Turntable Lab, using the browser's normal same-origin session. It requests product images from URLs supplied by the store. Store services, including Growave for wishlist actions, continue to operate under their own policies.

The extension saves only the selected availability filter and sort order in Firefox's local extension storage. Wishlist contents and cart data remain in page memory; they are not saved by the extension or sent to the extension developer. It does not read passwords, export cookies, or request access to other browsing history.

Adding to cart and removing a wishlist item happen only after the corresponding user click. Cart reads occur when the panel loads, after an add, on window focus, on a manual refresh, and every 30 seconds while the page is visible. It never submits checkout or purchases.

Removing the extension removes its ability to run. No separate account with the extension developer is required.
